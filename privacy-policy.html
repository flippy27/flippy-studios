Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Flippy Studios Publisher is a small program I run on my own computer to upload and schedule videos on my own YouTube channels. Nobody else uses it, and there is no sign-up.

YouTube API Services

The program uses YouTube API Services. Anyone using it agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it reads and writes

  • The name and id of my channels, so it knows where each video goes.
  • Status and public numbers (views, likes) of my own videos. I use them to plan the upload schedule and to catch problems early, like a removed or age-restricted video.
  • It uploads my videos, sets thumbnails, adds videos to my playlists, fixes titles or descriptions, and posts one comment from the channel under its own video.

It does not read anyone else's channels, comments or personal data.

Where the data lives

Login tokens and the data above stay in a database on my computer. I don't sell it or share it with anyone. Statistics are refreshed every 30 minutes, and anything older than 30 days is deleted automatically.

Removing access

Access to a Google account can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that the saved token stops working and I delete it together with the data stored for that channel. To ask for deletion, write to the address below.

Cookies

This site and the program don't use cookies or any tracking.
