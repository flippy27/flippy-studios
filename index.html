Flippy Studios Publisher

A small in-house tool I use to publish videos on my own YouTube channels: trivia, puzzles, Latin American history and legends, prayer videos, an original comic series and clip channels.

It prepares each upload, schedules it so videos are spread out during the day, and checks every half hour that nothing went wrong on the channels (removed or restricted videos). It works through the YouTube Data API (YouTube API Services) and only touches channels I own.

It isn't a public service, so there's nothing to sign up for here.
